Privacy Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Windage only collects what it needs to run your journal. We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address, your name if you enter it, and your Instagram handle if you add it for share cards.
  • Your journal: accounts, trades, prices, notes, reflections, plans, reviews, settings and chart screenshots you add.
  • Plan details: when your trial started, and your license key once you buy a plan.

What we don't collect

  • Card or bank details. Payments happen on Gumroad's checkout page, and we never see your card.
  • Your broker or prop-firm login. CSV imports are read in your browser.
  • Advertising or analytics tracking data.

How we use it

Only to run Windage for you: to sign you in, save and sync your journal across your devices, calculate your statistics and coaching, check your plan, and answer support messages.

Who processes it

  • Google Firebase stores your account and journal and handles sign-in.
  • Gumroad handles purchases as our Merchant of Record. When you activate a plan and each time the app opens, your license key is sent to Gumroad to check it's still active. Gumroad's privacy policy covers the details you give at checkout.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website. Fonts and some app libraries load from Google Fonts, jsDelivr and cdnjs, which receive standard web request information such as your IP address.

Who can see your journal

Only you, through your own login. Our database rules block everyone else from reading your data. We don't look at individual journals except when you ask for help and give permission.

On your device

Windage saves your colour theme and an offline copy of the app on your device so it loads quickly and works offline. You can clear it in your browser settings.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account exists. Delete it at any time in Settings → Delete my account. That permanently removes your journal, screenshots and login. Download a backup first if you want a copy. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Monthly plan, so cancel that first in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library).

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or ask a question about it, by emailing support@windagejournal.com. Depending on where you live, privacy laws may give you more rights.

Children

Windage is for adults. It isn't meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in an important way, we'll show a notice in Windage.
