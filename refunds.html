Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated November 20, 2026

Try everything in Windage free for 30 days before you pay. Because of that, payments are not refundable.

Free trial

New accounts start with a 30-day free trial with all features. You add a card on Gumroad when you start it, and you're not charged until the 30 days are up. Cancel before then and you pay nothing. If you don't cancel, your plan's first payment is taken when the trial ends and is not refunded.

Refunds

  • Monthly plan: payments are not refunded, including for partly used months.
  • 2-Year plan: payments are not refunded, including for partly used periods.
  • Exceptions: if you were charged twice, charged by mistake, or a technical problem on our side kept you from using Windage and we can't fix it, email us and we'll put it right, including a refund where that's fair. We'll also give any refund the law requires where you live.

Cancelling a plan

  • Cancel any time in your Gumroad library (gumroad.com/library → Manage membership → Cancel), or email support@windagejournal.com and we'll cancel it for you.
  • Once cancelled, you won't be charged again. You keep Windage until the end of the period you already paid for (the month, or the 2 years).
  • Your journal stays saved after your plan ends. You can still sign in to export or back it up, or delete your account.

Questions or problems with a charge

Please email support@windagejournal.com before disputing a charge with your bank. We usually reply within 2 business days. Payments are processed by Gumroad, our Merchant of Record.
